High-Current Buck Converter — 100A @ 1V

Power Electronics

Complete closed-loop power supply designed from schematic through PCB layout, assembly, and rigorous performance validation.

Project Overview

A comprehensive power electronics design project focusing on high-amperage step-down conversion with tight regulation, thermal management, and EMI suppression.

Engineering Highlights

  • Closed-Loop Control: Implemented real-time current regulation in C on microcontroller hardware with Hall-effect current feedback and op-amp signal conditioning.
  • Gate Drive & Switching: Configured half-bridge gate drivers driving high-current MOSFETs with optimized dead-time and anti-aliasing RC input filters.
  • Debugging & Troubleshooting: Diagnosed non-deterministic hardware PWM jitter using an oscilloscope, traced the root cause to timer conflicts, and successfully refactored to timer-controlled hardware PWM.
  • Precision: Achieved ~5% current measurement accuracy through calibration routines and software moving-average filtering.
