Solar Tracker — 2-Axis Gimbal System

Embedded

Embedded two-axis solar tracking platform engineered to boost photovoltaic panel efficiency by 5–10% using photoresistor feedback and servo-driven gimbals.

Project Overview

Developed at Western University (April 2024). Standard fixed photovoltaic installations achieve only ~20% energy conversion efficiency due to suboptimal solar incidence angles throughout the day. This project designed and validated an active 2-axis solar tracking and self-maintenance system.

Key Specifications & Architecture

  • Hardware Platform: Arduino UNO microcontroller, 5x7" photovoltaic test panel, dual servo motors (azimuth and elevation axes), 4 directional photoresistors (LDRs).
  • Control Loop: Differential analog light sensing with threshold hysteresis to prevent servo jitter; iterative PID parameter tuning for stable tracking response.
  • Tracking Efficiency: Achieved 82% tracking efficiency across simulated diurnal arcs.
  • Additional Mechanisms: Evaluated multi-augmentation strategies via AHP decision matrix, incorporating a self-cleaning wiper mechanism concept for dust and debris mitigation.
