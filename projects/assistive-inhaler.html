Assistive Inhaler Device

Biomedical

Human-centred multidisciplinary engineering project developing an accessible, patient-facing medication delivery and feedback device for visually impaired users.

Project Overview

Combining electrical, mechanical, and biomedical principles to solve real-world accessibility challenges in respiratory medication management.

Key Contributions & Design Process

  • User-Centred Requirements: Conducted stakeholder analysis and translated clinical/accessibility needs into rigorous engineering specifications.
  • Systems Integration: Co-designed tactile feedback mechanisms, acoustic dose confirmation, and robust mechanical enclosures.
  • Prototyping & Testing: Iterative rapid prototyping (3D printing and breadboard testing) to ensure ergonomic comfort and reliable operation.
  • Technical Communication: Delivered comprehensive design reviews and technical presentations under competitive constraints.
