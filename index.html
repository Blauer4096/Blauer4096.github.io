Spot Welder — High-Current Power Electronics

Power

A 100 A @ 1 V closed-loop spot welding supply from component selection through PCB layout and PI control firmware.

  • Modified buck converter: 18 V / 10 A input → 100 A @ 1 V output.
  • MOSFET and gate driver selection with thermal analysis; 15 kHz switching.
  • PI controller tuned via Ziegler-Nichols on Arduino with Hall-effect sensing.
  • Anti-aliasing filter, signal conditioning, and user interface with weld/squeeze timing.

High-Current Buck Converter — 100 A @ 1 V

Power

Complete closed-loop power supply designed from schematic through PCB layout, assembly, and performance validation.

  • Closed-loop current control in C on Arduino with Hall-effect sensing and op-amp signal conditioning.
  • MOSFET switching with half-bridge gate driver and anti-aliasing RC filters.
  • Diagnosed non-deterministic hardware PWM via oscilloscope, replaced with timer-controlled PWM.
  • ~5% current measurement accuracy via calibration and moving-average filtering.
