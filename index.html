High-Current Buck Converter — 100 A @ 1 V

Power

Complete closed-loop power supply designed from schematic through PCB layout, assembly, and performance validation.

  • Closed-loop current control in C on Arduino with Hall-effect sensing and op-amp signal conditioning.
  • MOSFET switching with half-bridge gate driver and anti-aliasing RC filters.
  • Diagnosed non-deterministic hardware PWM via oscilloscope, traced root cause, and replaced with timer-controlled PWM.
  • ~5% current measurement accuracy via calibration and moving-average filtering.
